03 / THE ENGAGEMENT

From scope
to clarity.

Work, authorization, reporting and timing are agreed before an engagement begins. The process makes each next step clear.

01

Discover & scope

Discuss the business context, assets, concerns and desired outcome. Agree the boundaries, contacts and authorization for the engagement.

02

Assess & investigate

Review the agreed systems and evidence. Validate findings, document limitations and keep the work within the approved scope.

03

Prioritize & report

Receive an explanation of the findings, their practical impact and the actions to prioritize. Reporting depth is agreed during scoping.

04

Remediate & review

Discuss remediation with your team. If included in the engagement, validate fixes and document remaining risks.

04 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

Your questions,
answered.

Where should I start?

Share what you need to protect, the systems involved and your main concern. An initial conversation helps define whether testing, exposure review, cloud assessment or incident investigation is the right next step.

Can you test systems I do not own?

Testing requires appropriate authorization from the system owner. Scope and written permission must be agreed before any assessment begins.

What is included in an assessment?

The scope can cover applications, APIs, infrastructure, cloud configurations or identity controls. The precise assets, depth, testing windows, reporting and any retest are agreed before the engagement.

How long does an engagement take?

Timing depends on asset count, complexity, access and the work required. Share your target date in the brief so scope and timing can be discussed together.

What should I share about a suspected incident?

Provide a concise timeline, the affected systems and the symptoms you observed. Use the initial brief to establish contact; evidence transfer and investigation access can then be agreed separately.

Can I request a report for my technical and business teams?

Discuss both audiences during scoping. A useful report connects validated technical findings with practical risk, priorities and remediation steps.

Does submitting a brief book a service?

The form prepares an email draft for you to review and send. An engagement begins only after scope, authorization, terms and timing are agreed.
